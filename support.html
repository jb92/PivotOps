Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started?

After installing the add-in, open Excel and go to the PivotOps tab in the ribbon. Click Dashboard, then open Settings (⚙) to enter your Azure DevOps Personal Access Token, organization, and project name.

Where do I get a Personal Access Token (PAT)?

In Azure DevOps, go to User Settings → Personal Access Tokens → New Token. Grant it the Work Items: Read & Write scope. Copy the token and paste it in PivotOps Settings.

Is my data sent to your servers?

No. PivotOps runs entirely in your browser. All API calls go directly from Excel to Azure DevOps. We do not operate any backend server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why does the Python engine take a moment to load?

PivotOps uses Pyodide (Python compiled to WebAssembly) for analytics and charts. The first load downloads ~30 MB from a CDN, which is then cached by your browser. Subsequent loads are much faster.

Can I use OAuth instead of a PAT?

Yes. In Settings, enter your Azure AD App Registration's Client ID and (optionally) Tenant ID. PivotOps will use MSAL to authenticate with automatic token refresh.

How do I uninstall and clear my data?

Remove the add-in from Excel (Insert → My Add-ins → right-click → Remove). This clears all locally stored settings including your PAT. You can also clear browser storage manually via developer tools.

The add-in shows "Not connected" — what do I do?

Open Settings (⚙) and verify your PAT is still valid (they expire). Also confirm your organization and project names are correct (case-sensitive).

System Requirements

  • Microsoft Excel (Microsoft 365, Office 2021, or Excel on the web)
  • ExcelApi 1.7 or higher
  • An Azure DevOps organization with work items
  • Internet connection (for Azure DevOps API and Pyodide CDN)
